# § 416.1082 Who conducts the hearings.

If a hearing is required, it will be conducted by the Department of Health and Human Services' Departmental Appeals Board (the Board).

> Source: [46 FR 29211, May 29, 1981, as amended at 62 FR 38455, July 18, 1997]
