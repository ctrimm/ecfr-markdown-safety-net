# § 416.1535 Entity eligible for direct payment of fees.

An entity is eligible for direct payment of an authorized fee if the entity:

(a) Has an Employer Identification Number;

(b) Has registered with us in the manner we prescribe;

(c) Has not been found ineligible for direct payment;

(d) Designates and maintains an employee who is registered as a representative in the manner we prescribe as a point of contact to speak and act on the entity's behalf;

(e) Accepts payment via electronic funds transfer; and

(f) Conforms to our rules.
