# § 457.230 FFP for State ADP expenditures.

FFP is available for State ADP expenditures for the design, development, or installation of mechanized claims processing and information retrieval systems and for the operation of certain systems. Additional HHS regulations and CMS procedures regarding the availability of FFP for ADP expenditures are in 2 CFR parts 200 and 300, 45 CFR part 95, subpart F, and part 11, State Medicaid Manual.
