# § 226.26 Program information.

Persons seeking information about this Program should contact their State administering agency or the appropriate FNSRO. The FNS website has contact information for State agencies at *https://www.fns.usda.gov/fns-contacts* and FNSROs at *https://www.fns.usda.gov/fns-regional-offices.*
