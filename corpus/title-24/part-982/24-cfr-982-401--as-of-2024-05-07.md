# § 982.401 Housing quality standards.

Link to an amendment published at 89 FR 38296, May 7, 2024.

As defined in § 982.4, housing quality standards (HQS) refers to the minimum quality standards developed by HUD in accordance with 24 CFR 5.703 for housing assisted under the HCV program or a HUD approved alternative standard for the PHA under 24 CFR 5.703(g).
