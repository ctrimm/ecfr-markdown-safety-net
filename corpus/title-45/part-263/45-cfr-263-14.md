# § 263.14 What methodology shall States use to allocate TANF costs?

States shall use a benefiting program cost allocation methodology consistent with the general requirements of 2 CFR parts 200 and 300, subpart E to allocate TANF costs.

> Source: [73 FR 42721, July 23, 2008, as amended at 81 FR 3020, Jan. 20, 2016; 89 FR 80071, Oct. 2, 2024]
